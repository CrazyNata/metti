# Metti — Google Play listing

## App name

Metti — AI Stylist

## Short description

Your personal AI stylist for thoughtful outfits and a calmer wardrobe.

## Full description

Metti is your personal AI stylist and a calm place for everything you wear.

Create a visual digital wardrobe, tell Metti where you are going, and get outfit ideas that fit your clothes, your personal style, the weather, and the occasion.

With Metti you can:

- Keep your wardrobe visual and organized in one place.
- Ask the stylist what to wear for work, a city day, date night, a trip, or anything in between.
- Get polished combinations built around the pieces you already own.
- Save the looks you love and keep track of the outfits you wear.
- Set your style, fit, size, colors, and everyday preferences so recommendations feel more personal.
- Switch between English and Russian whenever you like.

Metti is made for real life: fewer “what do I wear?” moments, less endless scrolling, and more confidence in the clothes already in your wardrobe.

Your wardrobe stays yours. Photos and personal details are kept private and available only to your account.
